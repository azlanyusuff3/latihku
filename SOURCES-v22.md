# LatihKu v22 — SRA Tahun 3–6 reference notes

LatihKu v22 uses public online material only as a **subject / format / difficulty reference**. It does not bundle or reproduce third-party commercial exam papers. The T3–T6 question bank in `data/<year>/sra.json` is original LatihKu content and the offline generator creates further variants from reviewed concepts.

Public references consulted during v22 design included:

- SRA Al-Najah — Tauhid Tahun 3 practice page: https://sites.google.com/jawi.edu.my/sraal-najah/tauhid/tahun-3
- SRA Al-Najah — Jawi Tahun 4 practice page: https://sites.google.com/jawi.edu.my/sraal-najah/jawi/tahun-4/latihan-jawi-tahun-4
- SRA Al-Najah — Bahasa Arab Tahun 5 sample objective/subjective questions: https://sites.google.com/jawi.edu.my/sraal-najah/bahasa-arab/tahun-5/soalan-soalan
- Koleksi SRA Selangor / KAFA JAIS index (Years 1–6 and PSRA): https://linktr.ee/koleksisra
- Public PSRA revision index: https://linktr.ee/latihtubipsra

Topic model used by LatihKu for SRA Years 3–6:
Tauhid, Fekah, Akhlak, Sirah, Tajwid, Jawi (including Imlak/Khat concepts where suitable), and Bahasa Arab.

Year 6 practice remains topic-separated inside LatihKu for focused revision even though PSRA-style papers may group subjects such as Tauhid+Fekah and Akhlak+Sirah.
